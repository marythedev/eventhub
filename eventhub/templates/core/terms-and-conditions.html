EventHub is a platform that allows users to create, promote, and sell tickets for events. We are currently operating in the prototype mode. All transactions are simulated, and no real payments are processed. EventHub does not organize, manage, or control any events listed on the Platform. All events are created and managed by independent users ("Organizers").

2.2 Data Management & Deletion

  • Accounts without associated events or orders can be permanently deleted.
  • Accounts with events or orders are "soft-deleted", meaning personal details are removed, and the account is deactivated but retained in the system to preserve transaction and event history.

  • Organizers are solely responsible for complying with all laws, permits, licenses, taxes, and regulations.

3.2 Event Data Management & Deletion

  • Events without sold tickets can be deleted.
  • Events with sold tickets cannot be deleted to preserve transaction history.

4. Payments

  • Ticket prices include Organizer-set taxes according to their local tax laws.
  • A service fee is added on top of the ticket price.
  • Organizers receive payouts after EventHub fees are deducted.

EventHub does not store or process any credit card details on our platform. All payment information is handled securely through our third-party provider Stripe, which is PCI-compliant. At this stage, our platform is in prototype mode, and all payments are simulated. No real transactions will occur.

5. Event Cancellation & Refunds

  • If an event is canceled or a refund is requested, Organizers or Buyers can contact EventHub. Our team will review your request and assist with processing refunds when applicable.
  • Service fees are non-refundable, as they cover Stripe’s processing fees.
